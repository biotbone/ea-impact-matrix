# Required documents

Generated by `python scripts/build_impact_matrix.py --intake`. The catalog lives in the script, so this file and the checks never disagree.

A matrix is only as good as the documents behind it. Five documents are required. The rest raise the quality of a specific sheet. Register every document in the `documents` list of the config with its status (Received, Requested, Not available, Not applicable). A missing required document does not stop the work, it changes it: the third column says how.

## Required

| Code | Document | Scope | Feeds in the workbook | What to look for | If it is missing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `PROPOSAL` | Proposal, business case or statement of work | Each initiative | Project profile (name, sponsor, scope, deadline, status, basis); Business value and Cost assessments; the questions the proposal asks the client go to 05 Open Questions | Scope in and out, stated benefits, stated risks, cost, duration, assumptions, questions put to the client | The initiative cannot be scored. Hold it and log what is needed on 05 Open Questions. |
| `ESTIMATE` | Cost and effort estimate: line items, hours by role, duration | Each initiative | Cost and duration cells, score per $100k on 02 Portfolio Summary, capacity on 03 Sequencing, Delivery confidence | Line items that add up to the total, hours that imply more than one person per role, consumption charges missing from the estimate | Score cost and confidence conservatively, leave score per $100k empty and log the gap. |
| `TARGET_STATE` | Target-state architecture, principles and strategy | Portfolio | Strategic alignment score, section 9 (capability, principles, gap closed), gates 1 and 2 | Target state, standards and principles, roadmap, capability map | Score strategic alignment conservatively and log the gap. Do not infer a target state from the proposals. |
| `LENS` | Sponsor confirmation of the prioritization lens (email or minutes) | Portfolio | 01 Scoring Model weights, Governance record on the Read Me (who agreed the lens, and when) | A named person, a date and the lens chosen: run cost, deadline risk, delivery speed, security or balanced | Do not score. The weights come before the scores. Ask the sponsor first. |
| `FUNDING` | Funding envelope or budget statement, or a written 'not yet defined' | Portfolio | 03 Sequencing (waves as approval tranches), Delivery confidence weighting | Amount, timing and approval path. If nothing is agreed, get that stated in writing. | Structure the waves as approval tranches and log the open envelope. |

## Recommended

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `CURRENT_STATE` | Current-state architecture or inventory of the affected systems | Portfolio | Dependencies, section 9 (gap closed), Risk reduction | Systems, owners, interfaces, shared platforms, naming and tenancy conventions | Dependencies are guesses. | `COST_BASELINE` | Current run-cost baseline: cost export, invoices, licence list | Each initiative or portfolio | 06 Savings Case, KPI baselines, Cost optimization score | Twelve months of cost for the affected services, by owner or tag | Never claim a saving. | `INCIDENTS` | Incident, risk and audit history | Portfolio | Risk reduction and Time criticality scores, 04 Risk Register | Recurring causes, outage counts, open audit findings, vendor end-of-support dates | Score risk reduction and time criticality conservatively and log the gap. |
| `SECURITY` | Security and compliance requirements, findings and review status | Each initiative | Security and compliance score, gates 3 and 8 | Control changes, data classification, regulatory duties, review outcomes | Score security conservatively. ## Sufficiency test

Before sending the workbook, every score must point to a document and a section, every cost to a line in an estimate, every KPI to a baseline source, and every owner to a name. `--check` tests the first part for you: it warns when a required document is not Received and when a score has no evidence.

## Intake request

Copy this to the client or sponsor:

> To score the proposed initiatives on one consistent basis we need the following. Please send what exists and tell us what does not.
>
> 1. Proposal, business case or statement of work (each initiative).
> 2. Cost and effort estimate: line items, hours by role, duration (each initiative).
> 3. Target-state architecture, principles and strategy (portfolio).
> 4. Sponsor confirmation of the prioritization lens (email or minutes) (portfolio).
> 5. Funding envelope or budget statement, or a written 'not yet defined' (portfolio).
>
> Helpful if available: current-state architecture or inventory of the affected systems; current run-cost baseline: cost export, invoices, licence list; incident, risk and audit history; security and compliance requirements, findings and review status; ownership and support model: named people, raci, org chart; resource and capacity plan: named roles and availability; vendor contracts, licences and renewal dates.
