# Proposal P1: Cloud cost visibility and tagging

Sponsor: Priya Narang, Head of Infrastructure. Delivery lead: K. Dunmore. Architect: R. Alder.

## Summary
Introduce a mandatory tagging standard and a monthly cost export so that every cloud cost can be traced to a named owner.

## Scope
In: tagging policy and enforcement, cost export pipeline, owner dashboards, owner training.
Out: any cost reduction work, chargeback to business units.

## Benefits
- 100% of cloud spend allocated to a named owner. Today about 60% is allocated, the rest is untagged shared spend.
- Gives the other initiatives a baseline against which a saving can be shown.

## Risks
- Owners ignore their dashboards.
- Shared services have no obvious owner.

## Duration
Two months. Cost and effort are in ESTIMATE-P1.
